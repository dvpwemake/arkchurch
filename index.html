🧠 Accessible Health Screening

MCI Cognitive Screening

ArkChurch partners with healthcare professionals to offer accessible Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) screenings to our community. MCI is an early stage of memory or thinking decline that can precede dementia, and early detection makes all the difference.

Our trained screeners conduct brief, non invasive cognitive assessments and connect participants with appropriate medical resources, follow up care, and family support. This service is affordable, completely confidential, and open to the community because caring for the mind is an expression of loving our neighbor.

  • Affordable, confidential cognitive health assessment (15 to 20 min)
  • Screening for memory, attention & reasoning
  • Referrals to neurologists & specialist services
  • Family support & caregiver resources
  • Available at select chapels, no appointment needed
